Steak

Easy at home steak recipe!

Ingredients

  • 4 1/2 lb. steak cubes
  • 1 10 oz. can of condensed french onion soup

Recipe steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
  2. Sear steaks in a large skillet over medium heat, 2-4 minutes each side
  3. Transfer steaks to a 9x13-inch baking dish. Pour condensed soup over top.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until tender and slightly pink in the center, about 1 hour
