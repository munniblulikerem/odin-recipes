Pig Pickin" Cake

A delicious and creamy cake!

Ingredients

Cake:

  • 1 package yellow cake mix
  • 1 can mandarin oranges with juice
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oli

Frosting:

  • 1 package frozen whipped topping, thawed
  • 1 can crushed pineapple, drained
  • 1 package instant vanilla pudding mix

Recipe steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Prepare the cake: Mix together cake mix, mandarin oranges with juice, eggs, and oil. Pour batter into three greased and floured 8-inch round pans. Layers will be thin.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center of cake comes out clean, 25 to 30 minutes. Cool layers on wire racks.
  4. Make the frosting: Mix together whipped topping, crushed pineapple, and instant pudding mix.
  5. Spread frosting onto the cooled cake layers. Stack layers; frost top and sides. Refrigerate until ready to eat.
  6. Slice and enjoy!
